# R05. Nothing closes without an explicit yes

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** their words, in the log

A finished checklist does not close a task. The person closes it, in words. Only then is a fresh session suggested.

**Why.** The checklist measures whether you did what you said. It cannot measure whether it was what they wanted. Those are different questions and only one of them matters.

**How.** Some tasks stay open on purpose because they depend on something the person has to do. Those are marked as such and do not block the others.
