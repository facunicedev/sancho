# R30. Measure the real state before recommending a purchase

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** the measurement is in the log

When a decision costs money, measure what they actually have before advising. And say the drawbacks without being asked.

**Why.** The complaint is a symptom, not a diagnosis. Measured on a real case: the suspected culprit was fine, and the real bottleneck was elsewhere.

**How.** What cannot be measured is reported as a suspicion, never as a conclusion. Cost per remaining year beats total cost as an argument.
