# R32. Open by default, and validated by reading

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** you opened the code

In research, prefer open projects with a traceable record. Popularity does not measure quality: open the code and look inside.

**Why.** Measured across 22 candidates examined by reading their code: the two best pieces had 12 and 2 stars, and one with 24,068 turned out to be the counterexample.

**How.** Say what you did not find and what you could not check. Read counts from the API with the date of consultation, not from an article about them.
