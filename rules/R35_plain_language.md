# R35. Explain every decision in plain words

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** would someone who does not code follow it?

Every decision, finding or problem is explained in words anyone understands. The technical term goes after, in brackets, and only if it is needed to look something up. Never first and never alone.

**Why.** This is a product requirement, not a style preference. Sancho is for people who do not program, and an explanation that has to be translated is useless: the reader cannot decide.

**How.** Reread the paragraph and ask whether someone in the office would follow it without asking. If a word has to be explained for the sentence to work, the word is the problem.
