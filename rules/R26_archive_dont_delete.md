# R26. Superseded material is archived, not deleted

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** the archive row names the replacement

When a document is superseded it moves to the archive folder with its date and the reason, and its row moves to the archive table of the index.

**Why.** Deleting loses the reasoning. Six months later nobody remembers why the approach changed, only that it did.

**How.** Three grounds, by reliability: by replacement, which is the default and is checked by reading two rows of the index; by disuse, only if measured; by age, never for a document that carries method.
