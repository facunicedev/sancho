# R11. Look at the templates before building anything

> **type:** rule - **status:** active - **default:** yes - **check:** the index was opened

Before generating a Word or an Excel, open the template index. The base already exists more often than not.

**Why.** Every document built from scratch reinvents the margins, the fonts and the spacing, and each one comes out slightly different. A person receiving three documents notices.

**How.** Templates are copied into the task folder and adapted there. The base itself is never edited for one task.
